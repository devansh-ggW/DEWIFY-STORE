DEWIFY / PRIVACY

Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. What this V1 collects

The static website stores your shopping bag in your browser's localStorage so it can survive a page refresh. The demo checkout asks for your name, phone number and delivery address only when you choose to prepare an order.

2. WhatsApp order flow

When you submit checkout, the website creates a pre-filled WhatsApp URL containing the order details you entered and your selected products. Your browser then opens WhatsApp. DEWIFY does not automatically send the message from this website.

3. Storage

The cart is stored locally in your browser. The current static V1 does not include an order database or server-side customer account system.

4. Third-party services

Opening WhatsApp transfers the relevant message data to WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own policies and terms. The website may also load fonts from Google Fonts unless those font links are removed from the project.

5. Security

This V1 is a frontend demo and is not a substitute for production security controls. A live store should use HTTPS, server-side validation, secure storage, access controls and appropriate payment and order-security measures.

6. Your information

For a production store, the operator should provide a clear process for questions, corrections, deletion requests and other rights required by applicable privacy law. This demo does not operate a customer account database.

7. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when DEWIFY adds real ordering, payments, analytics, customer accounts or fulfilment services.
