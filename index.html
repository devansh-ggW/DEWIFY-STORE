NEED TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I order?+

Add products to your bag, enter your delivery details and submit the checkout form. Your order is sent to DEWIFY through the store backend and saved in the order system.

How do I pay?+

Online payment is planned for the live checkout. This version records the order as pending and does not claim that a payment has been collected.

How does delivery work?+

Product-level delivery coverage and estimates depend on the fulfilment route and destination. Confirm the available shipping route before publishing a product for sale.

Can I cancel an order?+

Cancellation and refund terms should be published before accepting live payment. Order support can be handled through the DEWIFY contact channel.
