NEED TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I order?+

Add products to your bag, enter your delivery details and submit the checkout form. Your order is sent to DEWIFY through the store backend and saved in the order database.

Can I pay by UPI?+

The checkout records your selected payment method. Payment verification is handled separately and is not claimed by this checkout.

How does delivery work?+

Delivery terms are not defined for this demo storefront. Once real fulfilment is connected, delivery coverage, estimates and policies should be published here.

Can I cancel an order?+

Cancellation policy is not defined for this demo. For a live store, publish a clear cancellation and refund policy before accepting payment.
